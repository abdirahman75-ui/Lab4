01 / THE IDEA

Better together. Byte by byte.

This FSKTM IT Club homepage is a starting point for students interested in coding, web design, and creative problem-solving. Beginners can explore the basics while experienced students can share techniques and develop new project ideas.

The idea is simple: make something small, exchange feedback, and improve it together. You do not need to know everything before you start. Curiosity is a pretty good first step.
